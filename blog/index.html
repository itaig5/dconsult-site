Rate parity did not end. It split.

In the EEA, Booking.com no longer uses your prices on other channels for its ranking, its programmes or its own discount. Of the 13 versions of Expedia's published agreement I read, every one with a rate clause still requires properties in the EEA or Israel to give Expedia rates at least as good as on their own public website. Which rule binds you depends on the platform and on your country.
