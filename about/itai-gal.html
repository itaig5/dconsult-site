Itai Gal

Founder & CEO, D Consulting

Itai Gal founded D Consulting, a revenue management, distribution and travel-tech consultancy based in Tel Aviv-Yafo, Israel. Since 2016 the company has worked with hotels and vacation rentals across Israel and Europe, from strategy through to hands-on execution.

D Consulting built its own technology: Pacer, a self-learning revenue management system that forecasts demand and recommends pricing for every property, every week; and OpenBook, reservation analytics that reads an export from virtually any property-management system or channel.

On this site Itai writes about hotel distribution and revenue management, for people who run properties.
