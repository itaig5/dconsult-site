Proprietary technology

We couldn't find the tools we needed. So we built them.

Most consultancies work with whatever the market hands them. We kept hitting the same limits — so we built our own systems. They learn from every property, every week, and get sharper the longer they run.

Pacer

Revenue management that learns.

A self-learning revenue system that forecasts demand and recommends pricing for every property, every week — then audits its own recommendations to find where it was wrong.

  • Forecasts from your own history — pickup curves built per property from multi-year on-the-books data, not generic market averages.
  • Regime-aware — it re-weights seasons when the market shifts, so one disrupted year doesn't poison the model.
  • It critiques itself — a dedicated audit layer scores past recommendations and recalibrates automatically.
  • Runs on schedule — the weekly pipeline runs in the cloud and arrives as a decision-ready dashboard.
OpenBook

Your booking history already holds the answers.

Reservation analytics that reads an export from virtually any property-management system or channel, and turns it into the picture most owners never get to see.

  • Source-agnostic — it detects the format and normalises it, so data from different systems finally becomes comparable.
  • The questions that matter — channel mix, lead time, length of stay, room-type performance and seasonality.
  • Cancellations, quantified — where revenue is actually lost, by channel and by booking window.
  • Runs in your browser — the analysis happens on your machine; the data doesn't go anywhere.

Automated reporting
